# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes demonstrating empathy, respecting differing
viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility
for mistakes, and focusing on the health of the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insults, personal or
political attacks, publishing others' private information without permission,
and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate professionally.

## Enforcement and scope

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently ban participants for inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful conduct. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing
the project publicly.

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the organization contact
methods. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with respect for the
reporter's privacy and safety.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
